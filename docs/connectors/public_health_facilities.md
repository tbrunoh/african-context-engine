# Connector: public_health_facilities

**What it gives ACE.** Name, type, ownership, first level area and position of 49,459 public health facilities across the 13 countries. It feeds `nearest_facility`.

**Source.** Maina J, Ouma PO, Macharia PM, Alegana VA, Mitto B, Fall IS, Noor AM, Snow RW, Okiro EA (2019). A spatial database of health facilities managed by the public health sector in sub Saharan Africa. Scientific Data 6: 134. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0142-2. Assembled by the KEMRI-Wellcome Trust Research Programme from national master facility lists.

**Access.** No key. The spreadsheet on HDX (`health-facilities-in-sub-saharan-africa`), read with the standard library.

**Licence.** CC BY 4.0.

**Limits, stated in every answer.** Public facilities only. The list dates from 2019. It has no phone numbers, opening hours or services, so ACE never claims them; every answer ends with the country's verified emergency number.

**Refresh.** Quarterly, to pick up a new release.

**Fallback.** The V2 design adds national registries where they are open (Kenya Master Health Facility List) and healthsites.io in its own ODbL store, to add private facilities and services.
