# Governance

ACE is built to become shared infrastructure, so its governance is designed to move from a founding team to neutral stewardship as adoption grows.

## Stage 1: founding maintainers (now)

- The founder, Tom Bruno, and the founding maintainers review and merge changes and publish releases. The founder holds the ACE trademark until it moves to a neutral steward.
- Decisions are made in public issues and pull requests. Maintainers aim for consensus and record the reasoning for any decision taken without it.
- Anyone who makes sustained, high quality contributions can be invited as a maintainer.

## Stage 2: technical steering committee

Triggered when two or more mobile operators, governments or large partners run ACE in production.

- A technical steering committee (TSC) of up to nine seats: maintainers, operators, universities or research institutes, and government or public health bodies, with no single organization holding more than two seats.
- The TSC owns the roadmap, the context pack specification, the safety policy and the release process.
- Changes to licenses, the specification or the safety policy need a two thirds TSC vote and a public comment period of at least 14 days.

## Stage 3: neutral foundation

Triggered when contributors from several organizations maintain core components.

- The project, its trademarks and its infrastructure move to a neutral foundation, following the path the Model Context Protocol took when it joined the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation in December 2025.
- The commercial verified tier and hosted service stay with licensed operators of the ACE Verified program under terms the foundation publishes.

## Safety and data councils

From Stage 2, two standing councils advise the TSC:

- **Safety council**: clinicians, agronomists and lawyers who approve escalation policies, scope limits and the review standard for safety critical facts.
- **Data council**: representatives of farmers, patients and community organizations who review consent flows, data use and revenue sharing for contributors.
