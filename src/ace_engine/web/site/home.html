How ACE answers

Safety first, sources always, every phone.

01

The safety rail runs first

A message about bleeding in pregnancy, poisoning, violence, a child in danger, self harm, fire, drowning, a stabbing or a road accident gets the checked national numbers for that emergency, in the person's language. No model is involved, and the rail never waits on the network.

02

Checked and cited

Every answer comes from a knowledge pack or a dated public dataset, and names its source, licence and date. When ACE has nothing checked to say, it says so instead of guessing.

03

Built for every phone

USSD, SMS and voice reach phones with no data and no app. WhatsApp and RCS add the sources under every answer. Assistants and services connect through the API and MCP.

Honest about what is new

Every language after English is a preview.

ACE's translations are drafts until clinicians and fluent speakers review them, so each language after English carries a preview label until its review lands. The emergency numbers are checked against published public sources and do not depend on any translation.
