Try ACE

A phone that behaves like the real channels.

Pick a country and a language, then dial the USSD menu, send an SMS, read a voice call's script, or chat as on WhatsApp or RCS. Every answer opens its sources.

A demonstration. Emergency numbers are real and checked against published sources. No person monitors these messages and no one will call back. In an emergency, call the numbers ACE gives.

Your session is private

The phone never asks for a number. Your browser gets a random session that ACE keeps for 24 hours after your last message, so a two step question works. Send FORGET, or use the link under the phone, to delete it now.

What each channel shows

USSD counts the 182 characters a screen carries, fewer in Ge'ez and Arabic script. SMS counts segments and the alphabet they travel in. Voice shows the script a call would speak and whether a voice exists for the language.

Languages in preview

Every language after English is a draft that no fluent speaker has reviewed yet. If you speak one, your review makes ACE safer: open an issue or a pull request on GitHub.
