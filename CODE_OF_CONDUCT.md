# Code of conduct

ACE adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and language, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant apply.
